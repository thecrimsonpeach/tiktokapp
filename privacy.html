Privacy policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Crimson Peach is a private publishing tool used by one person, the poet Lena Cho, to post her own poems to her own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, X, Threads, Bluesky and TikTok, and to read back how those posts performed.

What the tool accesses

  • Only accounts that Lena owns and has connected herself.
  • Basic profile information for those accounts, such as the display name and username, to confirm which account is connected.
  • Aggregate figures for her own posts and accounts: views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves and follower counts.
  • Permission to upload and publish her own posts and videos to her own accounts.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not sign in anyone else, and nobody else can connect an account to it.
  • It does not collect, store or process personal information about anyone who views, likes or comments on the posts.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not run ads, send messages or track anyone across sites.

Where data is kept

Post texts, performance figures and the access tokens each platform issues are stored only on Lena's own computer. Tokens are used solely to publish her posts and read her own figures, are refreshed automatically, and can be revoked at any time in each platform's own settings.

Data deletion and contact

The tool holds no data about anyone other than its owner. To have anything related to you removed, or to ask about this policy, write to crimsonpeachlena@gmail.com; requests are handled within 30 days.
